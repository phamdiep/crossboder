Singapore

Payment Infrastructure — Singapore

⚡
FAST & PayNow
Real-time retail payments; PayNow enables mobile/NRIC addressing. Linked to PromptPay (TH), UPI (IN), Malaysian DuitNow.
🔗
SGQR & SPaN
Unified QR standard. MAS/ABS consolidating into Singapore Payments Network (SPaN) — merging PayNow, FAST, GIRO, SGQR under one platform.
🏦
SWIFT & Correspondent Banking
SG is a major SWIFT hub; gpi enables end-to-end tracking, same-day value for B2B/wholesale high-value transfers.
🧪
MAS Innovation (Ubin+, BLOOM)
Wholesale CBDC pilots; BLOOM enables programmable settlement using tokenized bank liabilities and regulated stablecoins.

Vietnam

Payment Infrastructure — Vietnam

🏃
NAPAS 24/7 & Interbank
National real-time ACH switch; processes domestic instant transfers and supports cross-border QR linkages (TH, LA, KR).
📲
VietQR & VietQR Global
National QR standard linked to NAPAS. VietQR Global enables VN consumers to pay via QR abroad; expanding to CN, JP, SG, IN.
💳
MoMo, ZaloPay & E-Wallets
Now official payment instruments under SBV regulation; MoMo has 31M+ users; cross-border QR usage recognized for travelers.
🌐
SBV Regulatory Sandbox
Active fintech sandbox; Law on Credit Institutions 2024 and updated payment intermediary rules open new corridors for licensed operators.

Deep Technology

Core Technology Building Blocks

📋
ISO 20022
Rich-data payment hub. pacs.008 messages carry full remittance info; structured UETR tracking; better AML/compliance.
⛓️
DLT & Tokenized Rails
Tokenized settlement via Ripple, MAS BLOOM, BIS Nexus. Atomic settlement removes nostro/vostro prefunding costs.
🤖
AI/ML Compliance
Real-time AML, sanctions screening, velocity checks embedded in payment flow. <500ms inline compliance decision.
🔄
FX Engine
Real-time SGD/VND rate streaming; pre-conversion quote lock; transparent FX margin disclosure per MAS/SBV requirements.
🔌
API Gateway & Orchestration
RESTful + ISO 20022 dual-mode APIs. Smart routing: selects fastest/cheapest rail per transaction (instant vs SWIFT vs DLT).
🔐
PII Tokenization
Data minimization across borders; tokenized identifiers comply with ASEAN cross-border data flow and VN localization rules.

Architecture

Three Architecture Options

Option A
SWIFT-Centric
  • SWIFT gpi as primary rail
  • Local payout via NAPAS/wallets
  • Lower build complexity
  • Higher cost & slower for retail
⚠ Not optimal for retail
✅ Recommended
Option B
Instant-Rail First Hub
  • FAST/PayNow (SG) + NAPAS/VietQR (VN)
  • SWIFT for high-value B2B fallback
  • Embedded FX + compliance
  • Supports all 3 use cases
⚡ Best cost/speed balance
Option C
B + Tokenized Overlay
  • Option B + Ripple/BLOOM rails
  • Atomic settlement for wholesale
  • Higher build complexity
  • Future-proof for CBDC era
🔭 Strategic long-term

Regulatory & Compliance

Key Regulatory Considerations

🇸🇬 MAS (Singapore)
  • Major Payment Institution (MPI) licence required
  • PS Act 2019 — digital payment tokens, e-money
  • MAS TRM notices for technology risk
  • SGFIU AML/CFT reporting obligations
🇻🇳 SBV (Vietnam)
  • Payment intermediary licence / agency banking
  • Law on Credit Institutions 2024
  • Circular 40 — e-wallet & QR regulations
  • Data localization for personal data of VN residents
🌏 Cross-Border Data
  • ASEAN cross-border data flow framework
  • PII tokenization to minimize data transfer
  • PDPA (SG) + PDPD (VN) dual-compliance
🛡️ AML & Sanctions
  • Real-time OFAC/UN/EU/MAS sanctions screening
  • End-to-end monitoring — <500ms inline decision
  • Velocity & behavior analytics (AI/ML)
